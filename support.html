Getting started

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later. No account or internet connection is needed to use the app.

How do I redact a document?

Drop a PDF, Word document, Excel workbook, image or text file onto Blot Genie. Choose Review to see what it found. Turn off anything you want to keep, then choose Save Copy. Your original file isn't changed.

What does Copy for AI do?

It copies the reviewed text so you can paste it into a chat. Each hidden detail becomes a label such as [Person 1], and the same name gets the same label everywhere. You choose where to paste it.

Can I redact a whole folder?

Yes, with Pro. Choose Redact a Whole Folder…. Each file is saved as a new reviewed copy and the originals are left alone.

Checking your redactions

Something private wasn't hidden

Automatic detection can miss things, especially unusual layouts, handwriting and text inside pictures. Always check the preview before you share. To hide something it missed, select it and choose Hide selection, or Hide all occurrences to hide it everywhere in the document.

Something ordinary was hidden

Turn it off in the review list before you save. Your choice applies to that document only.

My Word document looks different after saving

A reviewed Word copy is rebuilt from the text, so the layout can reflow. If the layout matters, save as PDF instead. A reviewed PDF keeps the page layout with the black bars.

Is the hidden text really gone?

Yes. Saved PDFs are flattened, so the text under each bar is removed, not just covered. Still, check the saved copy before you share it.

Plans

What's free, and what do Plus and Pro add?

Free includes 3 saved documents and 3 Copy for AI uses a month. Plus raises that to 20 of each for $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Pro is unlimited for $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year, and adds whole-folder redaction and redaction certificates. Monthly allowances reset on the first day of each month.

I subscribed on another Mac

Your plan is tied to your Apple Account. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and it unlocks automatically. If it doesn't, choose Restore Purchases.

How do I change plans or cancel?

In the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, then Subscriptions. From there you can switch between Plus and Pro, or cancel. Apple's step-by-step guide is here.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
