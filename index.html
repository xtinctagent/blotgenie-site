Questions

Does it catch everything?

No tool does. Blot Genie finds most private details automatically and shows you each one, so you make the final check before you save.

Can you see my files?

No. Blot Genie has no account system and no server that receives documents. Your files, the redacted copies and the names inside them stay on your Mac.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. The AI model is built into the app and runs on your Mac. Nothing you open is sent anywhere, so it can't be used for training.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The detection model is inside the app, and it works with Wi-Fi switched off.

Which files can I use?

PDF, Word, Excel, PNG, JPEG and plain text files.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later.

How much does it cost?

Blot Genie is free to download. Student and Professional are optional subscriptions that add more features, and the first 100 people to ask get the upgrade free.
