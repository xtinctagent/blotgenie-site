Privacy Policy

Blot Genie works on your Mac. Your documents never come to us.

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short versionWe don't collect your documents, your redactions or any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The Mac App Store version of Blot Genie has no permission to connect to the internet.

Your documents

Blot Genie reads the documents and images you open so it can find and hide private details. All of that happens on your Mac:

  • Detection uses an AI model that is built into the app and runs on your Mac.
  • Text in images and scanned pages is read with Apple's on-device text recognition.
  • Deep Scan, where available, uses Apple's on-device language model.

We never receive your documents, the names or numbers found in them, or your redaction choices. Nothing is sent to us or to an AI service.

What stays on your Mac

  • Review decisions stay in the open window. Closing the window discards them.
  • Saved copies go where you choose. If you save to a folder that another service syncs, such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, that service handles the file under its own terms.
  • Copy for AI puts the reviewed text on your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you, and that service's own privacy policy applies to what you paste.
  • Settings, such as whether you've seen the welcome screens, are stored locally. So is the count of free saves and Copy for AI uses for the current month.
  • Temporary files are created during some conversions and stay inside the app's private storage on your Mac.

Purchases

Blot Genie Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details, and Apple gives us only sales reports that don't identify you. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your purchase.

Crash reports

Blot Genie doesn't send crash reports itself. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us

If you contact support@blotgenie.com, we use your email address and message only to reply. Please don't send us your private documents. We'll never ask for them.

This website

blotgenie.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Blot Genie doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions go to support@blotgenie.com.
